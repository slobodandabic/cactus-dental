Questions

Good to know before your visit

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take. You can fill out the new patient forms online after you book, which saves about 15 minutes in the office.

How much will my visit cost?

We always tell you the price before treatment. With in-network insurance, cleanings and exams are usually covered at 100%. Without insurance, our new patient special is $99, and the Wren Smile Plan starts at $29 a month.

Do you see kids?

Yes. We see children from their first tooth (around age one) and up. Parents are always welcome to stay in the room.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Tell us when you book and we'll schedule extra time. We offer noise-cancelling headphones, a weighted blanket, nitrous oxide and oral sedation. You can raise your hand to pause at any time.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We accept CareCredit and Sunbit, with 0% financing on treatment over $500 for qualified patients, and all major cards and HSA/FSA cards.
